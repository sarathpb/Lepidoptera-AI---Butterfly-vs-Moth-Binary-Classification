Lepidoptera Taxa Comparison Guide

Butterfly (Rhopalocera)

  • Antennae: Clubbed / knobbed swollen tips
  • Resting Posture: Wings folded vertically upright
  • Body Texture: Slender, smooth, streamlined
  • Activity: Diurnal (sunlight active)
  • Frenulum: Absent (overlapping wing mechanism)

Moth (Heterocera)

  • Antennae: Feathery (bipectinate) or threadlike
  • Resting Posture: Wings folded flat, tent-like
  • Body Texture: Stout, dense woolly fur/setae
  • Activity: Predominantly nocturnal
  • Frenulum: Present (bristle-hook wing lock)
